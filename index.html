LUXEMBOURG ARMWRESTLING COMPETITION REGISTRATION
MUST-READ INFORMATION

WELCOME ATHLETES

Thank you for your support and for taking part in the Luxembourg Armwrestling Competition. Please read this section carefully before registering: everything you need to know for your registration and participation is written here.

EVENT

This year will be the 7th edition of the international Luxembourg Armwrestling Competition, taking place on 20 April 2027.

REGISTRATION FEE

25€ per arm, per category.

Payment is made in cash on-site during registration / weigh-in times.

You may register in multiple categories.

LOCATION

Sport Hall Schuttrange, Munsbach, Luxembourg. Google Maps location will be available on the website.

PARTICIPANTS & CATEGORIES

Around 100–150 athletes.

18 categories.

REGISTRATION & WEIGH-IN TIMES

  • 19 April 2027: 18:00 – 20:00
  • 20 April 2027: 09:00 – 11:30
  • Payment is made in cash on-site during these times.
  • Athletes must complete registration, payment, ID check, and weigh-in before competing.
  • A possible Open Category among podium athletes from pro categories may be decided on the day of the event (free of charge).

EVENT SCHEDULE

  • 11:30 – 13:00: lunch break
  • 13:00: competition starts
  • Left arm starts first and is estimated to last 1–2 hours.
  • After a short break, right arm starts and is estimated to last 1–2 hours.
  • Estimated finish: around 17:00

CATEGORY RULES

  • Multiple entries: athletes may register in several categories.
  • Category changes: some categories may be changed or added depending on demand. Use the suggestion box at the end of the form if needed.
  • ID check: an ID card will be required on the day of the tournament to verify registration details.
  • Restrictions: some categories are limited by sex, age, weight, or practice time.
  • A 0.1 kg tolerance is accepted due to normal scale variation and daily conditions.

AMATEUR CATEGORY

  • Reserved for low-experience athletes starting in the sport.
  • You must have less than 1 year of practice in a club.
  • You must not have made a podium in any previous competition.
  • Club information may be requested and club presidents may be contacted to verify eligibility.

WARM-UP & AFTERPULL AREA

  • A defined area will be provided with official tables to warm-up.
  • Before and during the competition time, the warm-up zone is strictly reserved for athletes wearing the official event badge.
  • After the competition (approx. 5pm), tables in the warm-up zone are available in priority to badge-carrying athletes for afterpull.
  • Public access after the competition may be possible only with acceptance of the club and organizer liability discharge in case of injury.
